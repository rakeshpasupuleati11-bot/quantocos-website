The friction

Why this work stalls in a normal supplier model.

01

The page and the campaign disagree

Creative and digital are bought separately, so the landing page undercuts the promise in the ad.

02

Measurement arrives after the spend

Analytics is instrumented once and never checked against the questions the team actually asks.

03

Content operations depend on one person

The calendar works until the person holding it leaves, then the cadence stops.

The deliverables

What the pod actually makes.

The list is a catalog, not a promise of volume. The brief decides which items run in a given month.

Web

Landing pages

Designed, built and instrumented as one deliverable.

Email

Lifecycle email

Sequences, templates and the rules that keep them consistent.

Search

SEO content operations

Briefs, publishing cadence and internal linking as a repeatable process.

Measurement

Analytics setup

Events, definitions and dashboards tied to the questions the team asks.

CRO

Experiment design

A prioritised test queue with a written readout for each result.

Operations

Content systems

Component libraries and editorial rules that outlive a single campaign.

How the month runs

From brief to release, with a gate between each step.

The pod does not skip a gate because a deadline moved. It re-scopes at the next gate instead.

Brief

The property, audience, channels and deadline are written into one scope.

Direction

The pod agrees the treatment and the production route at Gate 2.

Production

Specialists build the items; internal QA checks them at Gate 3.

Review and release

You review at Gate 4, then Gate 5 releases the files and sources.

Where it fits

A marketing team whose page and campaign are built by different suppliers.

The Digital Pod fits when the buyer journey crosses several surfaces and nobody owns the whole path. It also suits a team that wants measurement defined before spend.

  • A launch that needs pages and email together
  • A content operation that must survive staff changes
  • Analytics that answers a real question

Do you build in our CMS?

Yes, where the platform allows a clean component build. The brief records the platform, access and handover requirements before work starts.

Can the pod work with our analytics stack?

Yes. We instrument against the events you already collect, and we document every definition we add.

Is SEO content included?

Content operations are included; ranking outcomes are not promised. Search results depend on factors outside any supplier's control.
