The model

One subscription. One pod. One credit ledger.

GC³ stands for Global Cloud Capability Center. It is the operating model behind every track: a named pod, a published credit rate and five gates that approve each deliverable.

How a pod is staffed

A named manager, specialists, and agents that carry the repeatable work.

A pod is assembled once against the brief and stays together while the engagement runs. The manager holds the context, the specialists produce, and AI agents handle the repeatable steps under review.

  • One manager accountable for the account
  • Specialists from the tracks you bought
  • AI agents inside a documented workflow
  • Internal QA before your review

The credit ledger

One rate, one balance, visible every month.

Every deliverable is priced in credits at $25. The monthly statement shows what was drawn, what remains and what was rebalanced, so the commercial conversation is about the work rather than the invoice.

  • $25 per credit across every pod
  • Published pools of 120, 240 and 360 credits
  • Unused credits visible before the next cycle
  • No setup fee and no exit fee

The five gates

Every deliverable passes the same five points.

A gate is a decision, not a status update. Nothing moves to the next stage without one.

G1 · Brief approval

Scope, deliverables and a credit estimate are agreed in writing.

G2 · Direction selection

You choose the route before production starts.

G3 · Internal QA

The pod checks the work before you are asked to review it.

G4 · Client review

Your review window; comments are logged and resolved.

G5 · Go-live

Files, sources and handover notes are released.

What GC³ is not

The model is easier to trust when its edges are explicit.

Commercials

Not an agency retainer

You buy a credit pool against a brief, not a block of unspecified hours.

Staffing

Not a freelancer marketplace

Specialists are assembled into one pod with one manager, not matched per task.

Output

Not a software licence

The work is produced for you and assigned to you; the platform is not the deliverable.

Scope

Not an open-ended queue

Scope is written at Gate 1 and reported against each month.

Timing

Not a fixed turnaround promise

Timing is agreed in the brief because access and formats vary.

Limits

Not a substitute for licensed advice

Legal, tax and regulated counsel stay with qualified professionals.

Questions

Questions about the operating model.

If something is not covered, ask before you commit.

Is there a minimum term?

No. The subscription is month to month, with written notice before the next billing date.

What happens to unused credits?

Unused credits are visible on the monthly statement. Rollover and refund treatment is set out in the engagement letter for your plan.

Can the pod change tracks mid-engagement?

Yes, at a billing boundary. The pod rebalances at the next cycle so scope and credits stay aligned.
