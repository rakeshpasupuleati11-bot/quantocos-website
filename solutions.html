Scoping a pod

A custom pod is a blend of tracks with one manager.

When the outcome crosses tracks, the pod is scoped as one brief with one manager and one credit pool. The monthly figure is agreed in writing before the first deliverable.

  • Tracks are selected against the brief, not sold as bundles
  • Credits are drawn at the published $25 rate
  • Scope changes are approved before work starts
  • The pod can be rebalanced at the next billing date

How credits work

One rate, one pool, visible before work starts.

Every deliverable is priced in credits at $25. The published pools of 120, 240 and 360 credits set the monthly subscription; the statement shows what was drawn and what remains.

  • $25 per credit, published and fixed
  • Credits are estimated in writing at Gate 1, before production
  • Unused credits stay visible on the monthly statement
  • The 14-day pilot ($1,500, 60 credits) credits back against the first month

Shared by all four solutions
