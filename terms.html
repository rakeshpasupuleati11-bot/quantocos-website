The terms

What governs the work.

The commercial facts match the pricing page and the engagement letter. The contract pack is available before a commercial conversation.

What these terms cover

These terms govern the use of this website. They are not the contract for services: client work is provided under a written engagement letter and, where applicable, a master services agreement.

If these terms and the engagement letter disagree, the engagement letter prevails for the services it covers.

Plans and credits

Creative is published at $3,000 a month for 120 credits, Digital at $6,000 for 240 credits and Strategy at $9,000 for 360 credits. Interactive, Automation and REMA U are scoped per client in a written brief.

A credit is priced at $25 on every published pod. The engagement letter records the credit pool, the overage treatment and the notice period for the plan you buy.

No setup fee and no exit fee applies. The subscription is month to month, with written notice before the next billing date.

The Pilot Sprint

The Pilot Sprint is 14 days with 60 credits for $1,500. It ends on its own and carries no lock-in.

Subscribe to a pod within 14 days of the pilot ending and the $1,500 is credited against the first month. Unused pilot credits are refunded.

Scope, gates and review

Work starts only after the brief is approved at G1, and a change to scope is priced in credits and approved before the work begins.

G2 selects the direction, G3 is the pod's internal quality check, G4 is the client review window and G5 releases the files. The engagement letter records the review window for your account.

G4 is a review window, not a delivery promise. A missed approval pauses the work rather than consuming credits silently.

Intellectual property

On full payment, copyright and intellectual property in the delivered work assign to the client, and source files are handed over at G5.

Tools, methods, frameworks and pre-existing materials used to produce the work remain the property of Quantocos Global Systems LLP or their respective owners. A licence to use them for the delivered work is included.

Marks including GC³, Prism, Apara, April, Friday, CoBridge, Sigma, Qore, HPCF, ASTX, Caksh, QREK, QRT, D3 and the E2E framework belong to the LLP and may not be used without written permission.

AI use and assurance

AI agents may carry repeatable steps inside a documented workflow, with human review at the gates. They do not approve work, sign deliverables or replace the named pod manager.

Client material is not used to train a public model. The AI assurance note in the contract pack records the tools in scope and the review points for an engagement.

Confidentiality and data

Confidentiality runs both ways. A mutual NDA is available before the first call, and a data processing agreement is signed before any client data is shared.

The privacy policy describes what this website collects. Client data handling is governed by the processing agreement, not by the website policy.

Acceptable use of this site

You may read, quote and link to this site for legitimate business purposes with attribution. You may not scrape it at volume, attempt to bypass its security, or present its content as your own.

The site is provided as it stands. Prices and facts are checked and dated, but they do not create a contract until an engagement letter is signed.

Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

The liability cap, indemnities and insurance position for services are set out in the master services agreement and the engagement letter. This website does not restate them.

Term, termination and law

A subscription runs month to month and can be ended with written notice before the next billing date. The pilot ends at the end of its 14 days unless a pod is purchased.

On termination, delivered work and assigned IP remain with the client, and undelivered credits are treated as the engagement letter records.

The governing law and jurisdiction for a client contract are stated in the master services agreement. Nothing on this site selects a jurisdiction by implication.

Questions about these terms

Write to hello@quantocos.com and the question will be answered in writing. Procurement documents, including the NDA, the security questionnaire and the AI assurance note, are available on request before a commercial conversation.

These terms were last reviewed on 27 November 2026. A change is published here before it applies to a new engagement.
