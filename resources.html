Briefing checklists

Four lists that prevent most first-month rework.

01

Brand checklist

Marks, type, colour, tone and the examples of work you consider right.

02

Audience checklist

Who the work is for, what they already believe and what would change their mind.

03

Asset checklist

What exists, who owns it, what may be reused and where the files live.

04

Approval checklist

Who signs at G2 and G4, how fast, and what happens when they are away.

Glossary

The words this site uses, defined.

Shared definitions make a brief shorter. These are the terms that most often need one.

Pod

A named manager plus the specialists and agents assigned to your account. A pod is staffed against the brief, not against a job title.

Credit

The unit every deliverable is priced in. One credit is $25 on every published pod, so a 120-credit pool is $3,000.

Gate

A decision point with a named approver. There are five, from G1 brief approval to G5 go-live.

Stage

A phase of work inside the month. Stages run the work; gates approve each deliverable.

Brief

The written scope agreed at G1. It records the outcome, audience, deliverables, deadline and credit estimate.

Scope

What the pod will and will not produce in a given month. Changes to scope are approved before work starts.

Rebalance

The adjustment of tracks and credits at a billing boundary. It keeps the pod aligned to the current brief.

Statement

The monthly record of what was delivered, what was drawn and what remains. It is not an invoice.

Published pod

A pod with a fixed monthly price and credit pool. Creative, Digital and Strategy are published.

Scoped pod

A pod quoted per client. Interactive, Automation and REMA U are scoped because the build varies.

Pilot Sprint

A 14-day engagement of 60 credits for $1,500. It ends on its own, with no lock-in.

Internal QA

Gate G3, where the pod checks its own work before the client sees it. It is not the client review.

Review window

The time reserved for your G4 review. It is your window, not our delivery promise.

IP assignment

The transfer of delivered work to you under the engagement letter. Source files are handed over at G5.

A note on what is not published here.

We do not publish sample client results, named case studies or benchmarks we cannot verify. If a claim is not in a written brief, treat it as unproven.
