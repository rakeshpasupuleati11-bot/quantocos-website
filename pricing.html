Before a subscription

The Pilot Sprint: $1,500, 60 credits, 14 days.

The pilot is a real engagement with a real brief, sized so both sides can judge the fit. It ends on its own, with no lock-in and no conversion obligation.

  • Subscribe to any pod within 14 days of the pilot ending and the $1,500 is credited against your first month.
  • Unused pilot credits are refunded.
  • The pilot ends on its own — no lock-in.
  • Scope is agreed in writing before it starts.

Terms in summary

What the engagement letter says.

The full terms sit in the engagement letter. These are the commercial points a buyer usually checks first.

Billing

No setup fee. No exit fee. Month to month, with written notice before the next billing date.

Never charged as extras

  • Onboarding
  • Brand ingestion
  • Brief clarification
  • Asset handover
  • Monthly pod review

Change requests

A change to scope is priced in credits and approved before work begins. Nothing is added silently to an invoice.

What is not included

Media spend, third-party licences and travel are billed at cost where the brief requires them, with your approval.

Why are three pods priced and three scoped?

Creative, Digital and Strategy have repeatable monthly catalogs. Interactive, Automation and REMA U depend on systems, devices and production volume, so they are quoted per client.

Do unused credits roll over?

Rollover treatment is stated in the engagement letter for your plan. The monthly statement shows the balance either way.

Is the pilot price credited?

Yes. Subscribe within 14 days of the pilot ending and the $1,500 is credited against your first month. Unused pilot credits are refunded.
