The friction

Where a good product gets a bad explanation.

01

The site lags the release

Marketing pages describe a version of the product that shipped two quarters ago.

02

The demo is a sales artefact

Every call gets a bespoke walkthrough because there is no shared demo.

03

The story changes by audience

Investors, buyers and developers hear three different descriptions of the same product.

The deliverables

One product story, expressed for each audience.

The pod keeps a single source of truth and builds the formats around it.

How the work runs

Ship the story with the release.

The pod works to the release calendar, not to a separate marketing calendar.

Release review

What shipped, what changed and who needs to hear it are agreed.

Message update

Positioning and copy are revised where the release changes them.

Asset build

Pages, demo and launch assets are produced together.

Release

Everything goes live with the release at Gate 5.

Where it fits

A product team that ships regularly and has no owner for the explanation.

The pod fits when releases are planned enough to work against. It does not replace product management, engineering or investor relations.

  • A release calendar
  • Access to product and demo environments
  • A founder or product lead who can approve

Do you write technical documentation?

We produce documentation visuals, structure and editing. Deep reference documentation stays with engineering.

Can the demo use real product data?

Only with an approved environment and a data-handling note. Synthetic data is the default for public demos.

Can you support a fundraise?

We can build the narrative and the materials. We do not provide fundraising advice or introductions.
