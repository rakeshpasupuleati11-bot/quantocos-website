Industry · Retail and e-commerce

Seasonal demand, without the seasonal scramble.

Retail marketing runs on a calendar that cannot move. The range lands, the campaign opens and every product page, email and social asset has to be ready on the same day. A pod builds the system before the season, then runs it through the peak.

How the work runs

Prepare before the peak, run during it.

The pod front-loads the system so the season itself is execution, not invention.

Calendar lock

Launch dates and approval windows are agreed for the season.

System build

Templates, rules and content structures are signed off at Gate 2.

Production waves

Assets are produced in waves and checked ahead of each date.

In-season support

The pod handles cutdowns and corrections during the peak.

Where it fits

A retail or e-commerce team with a fixed promotional calendar and recurring content demand.

The pod fits when the range and the dates are known far enough ahead to build a system. It is not a substitute for merchandising or media buying.

  • A locked seasonal calendar
  • Product data and imagery access
  • A marketing owner who can approve waves

Can you handle peak volume?

Volume is agreed in the brief and measured in credits. If the peak needs more than the pod can absorb, we say so before the calendar is locked.

Do you produce product photography?

Image direction and asset production can be scoped. Physical shoots are quoted separately with crew and location.

Can you work with our e-commerce platform?

Yes, where the platform allows a clean template build. Access and handover are recorded at Gate 1.
