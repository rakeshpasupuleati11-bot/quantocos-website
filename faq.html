01 · Pricing

What it costs and how credits work.

What does a pod cost?

Creative is $3,000 a month for 120 credits, Digital is $6,000 for 240 and Strategy is $9,000 for 360. Interactive, Automation and REMA U are scoped per client.

What is a credit worth?

$25. The rate is the same on every pod, so a 60-credit pilot is $1,500 and a 240-credit pod is $6,000.

Are there setup or exit fees?

No. There is no setup fee and no exit fee, and the subscription is month to month with written notice before the next billing date.

What is the Pilot Sprint?

It is 14 days with 60 credits for $1,500. If you subscribe within 14 days of the pilot ending, the $1,500 is credited against your first month, and unused pilot credits are refunded.

Do unused credits roll over?

Rollover treatment is stated in the engagement letter for your plan. The monthly statement always shows the balance.

02 · The model

Pods, tracks, stages and gates.

What is GC³?

It is the Global Cloud Capability Center: one subscription model with six capability tracks, one credit rate and five approval gates.

Can I buy one track only?

Yes. Each track can run as a single-track pod, and the credit rate does not change.

What are the five gates?

G1 brief approval, G2 direction selection, G3 internal QA, G4 client review and G5 go-live. Every deliverable passes the same five.

Who works on my account?

A named manager and specialists from the tracks you bought. Named specialists work from India, overlapping your business day.

Do you use AI?

AI agents carry repeatable steps inside a documented workflow, with human review at the gates. They do not approve work or replace the named manager.

03 · Working together

Onboarding, review, change and fit.

How does onboarding work?

A kickoff session sets the outcome, approver and calendar, then the first brief is scoped at G1. Onboarding, brand ingestion, brief clarification, asset handover and the monthly pod review are not charged as extras.

How long is the review window?

G4 is your review window, agreed in the engagement letter and recorded per deliverable. It is not a delivery promise.

What happens if scope changes?

The change is priced in credits and approved before work starts. Nothing is added to an invoice without written approval.

Where do you work?

The operating geography is the US, UK, EU, UAE and ANZ. Some markets are served through independent delivery partners, for example Singapore.

When is a pod a bad fit?

A one-off task, no named approver, or work that needs regulated professional advice. In those cases we will say so.

04 · Legal and ownership

IP, privacy and the engagement letter.

Who owns the work?

Delivered work assigns to you under the engagement letter, and source files are handed over at G5.

Do you have certifications?

We do not claim certifications we do not hold. If a security questionnaire requires one, tell us early and we will answer it honestly.

Where are the full terms?

In the engagement letter, with a summary on the pricing page. The privacy policy and terms of service are published on this site.

How is my data handled?

Access is scoped to the work and logged, and sensitive steps keep a human approval point. The privacy policy sets out the detail.
