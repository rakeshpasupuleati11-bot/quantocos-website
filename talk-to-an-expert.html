What to bring

Three things make the session useful.

You do not need a finished brief. You need enough context for the pod to tell the difference between a one-month job and a six-month programme.

  • The outcome you are accountable for
  • The date it has to be true, and why
  • The constraints: brand, systems, approvers, budget

Who attends from your side?

The manager who would own the pod if it proceeds, plus a specialist from the lead track. You do not need to prepare a deck.

What if we are not ready to buy?

That is normal. The written view is still useful for an internal decision, and it is yours to keep.

Can the session be recorded?

Yes, with consent from everyone present. The recording is shared with you and not used in marketing without written permission.
