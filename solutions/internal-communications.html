Solution · Internal communications

Get the whole team telling the same story.

Internal communication fails quietly: the strategy is announced, then each function rewrites it for its own audience. A pod holds the message architecture and produces the assets that carry it, from leadership film to the manager pack.

The friction

Why internal communication stops landing.

01

Every function rewrites the message

The strategy is interpreted in each department until the versions no longer agree.

02

Leadership film is a one-off

A single video is produced for the town hall, then the next announcement starts from zero.

03

Managers get a slide, not a script

Front-line managers are asked to carry the message without the assets or the language to do it.

The deliverables

A message system your managers can actually use.

The pod builds the narrative once, then produces the formats each audience needs.

Foundation

Message architecture

One page the whole company can repeat without drifting.

Events

Onboarding library

Change communications

How the work runs

A quarterly narrative, produced monthly.

The rhythm matters more than any single asset. This is how the pod keeps it.

Narrative review

The quarter's story is agreed with the people who own the change.

Production set

Film, slides and manager assets are produced as one batch.

Internal QA

Consistency and accessibility are checked before release.

Rollout support

The pod supplies cutdowns and answers for the rollout week.

Where it fits

A company whose strategy is clearer in the boardroom than in the break room.

The pod fits when there is a real change to communicate and someone accountable for it. It is less useful when the message itself has not been decided.

  • A change programme with a date
  • A leadership team willing to be filmed
  • An internal owner who can approve

The practical questions, answered without jargon.

Do you need access to our intranet?

Usually not. We produce the assets and the copy; publishing stays with your internal team unless the brief includes platform work.

Can leaders be filmed remotely?

Yes. Remote capture is the default; in-person production is scoped separately with travel and crew.

How do you handle sensitive announcements?

Access is limited to the named pod and approver, and drafts stay inside the agreed review path. We do not publish anything externally.
