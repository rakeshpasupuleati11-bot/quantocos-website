Who it fits

US SMBs and mid-market teams with recurring work.

The model suits organisations that buy creative and digital work every month, across the US, UK, EU, UAE and ANZ. It is designed for owners and operators who want one accountable team rather than a list of suppliers.

  • An owner or operator who can approve work
  • Recurring demand, not a single asset
  • A brand or product that can share context
  • A decision rhythm the pod can plan against

Where a pod fits

Four conditions, checked before a proposal.

If one of these is missing, the honest answer may be a smaller engagement or a different supplier.

Recurring demand

There is enough work each month to keep a pod busy.

A named approver

One person can say yes at Gates 2 and 4.

Shareable context

Brand, product and audience material can be handed over.

A defined outcome

The result can be written down and measured.

Where it does not fit

Saying no is part of the model.

01

A single small task

One asset or one page does not need a monthly pod; the Pilot Sprint is the better entry point.

02

No approver inside the company

Without a named decision-maker, work stalls at Gate 2 and the credit pool burns.

03

Regulated advice

Legal, medical, financial and tax advice requires licensed professionals; the pod produces communication, not regulated counsel.

04

Work that needs permanent on-site presence

The pod works remotely with overlapping hours; continuous physical presence is outside scope.

Do you only work with US companies?

The site is written for US SMB owners, and the operating geography is the US, UK, EU, UAE and ANZ. If you are outside those markets, ask before briefing.

Is there a sector-specific price?

No. The three published pods carry one price, and the scoped tracks are quoted per client. Sector does not change the credit rate.

Can I start with the pilot in my sector?

Yes. The Pilot Sprint is available across sectors and gives 60 credits over 14 days for $1,500.
