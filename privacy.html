The policy

What happens to your data.

Short sections, each answering one question. Requests go to hello@quantocos.com.

Who we are

Quantocos Global Systems LLP, registered identifier ACY-3556, is the controller of personal data described in this policy. Write to hello@quantocos.com for any question or request about it.

This policy covers this website and enquiries made through it. Client data handled during an engagement is governed by the data processing agreement signed before any client data is shared.

What this site collects

The site is static. It does not create an account, set an advertising cookie or run an analytics script, because no analytics configuration has been approved.

The hosting provider may record standard request data such as IP address, user agent and the page requested, in order to serve and protect the site. That logging is operated by the host, not assembled into a profile by us.

If you use a form on this site, nothing is transmitted to a server. The form prepares a message in your own email application, and the details reach us only when you choose to send it.

What we do with an enquiry

An email to hello@quantocos.com is used to answer the enquiry, to prepare a scope if one is requested, and to keep a record of what was agreed. It is not added to a marketing list.

Where an engagement follows, the contact details are used to administer it: scheduling, approvals, invoicing and support. Invoicing details are held by the entity named on the engagement letter.

Lawful bases

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, an enquiry is handled on the basis of legitimate interests in responding to a business request, or on the basis of steps taken at your request before entering a contract.

Contract administration relies on the contract itself, and any legal or accounting obligation relies on the corresponding legal basis. Consent is used only where it is specifically asked for, and it can be withdrawn.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own for advertising, profiling or analytics. The only browser storage used is a preference for reduced motion, held locally in your browser and never sent to us.

If a cookie is ever introduced for measurement, it will be described here before it is switched on and will require consent where the law requires it.

Retention

Enquiries that do not become engagements are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Records tied to an engagement are kept for the period required by the engagement letter and by applicable accounting law.

Form data never reaches us unless you send the prepared email, so there is no form database to retain or delete.

Sharing

Personal data is shared with the hosting provider that serves this site, the email provider that receives hello@quantocos.com, and professional advisers where necessary.

Where an engagement names an independent delivery partner, the engagement letter identifies it and the data processing agreement records what it may process. Partners are not group offices, subsidiaries or agents.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

Your rights

Where UK or EU law applies, you can ask for a copy of your personal data, correction of it, deletion, restriction of processing, or portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Requests are answered in writing within one month, and identity may be confirmed first so that data is not released to the wrong person. If a request is refused, the reason will be given in writing.

You can complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work. We would prefer the chance to resolve it first, at hello@quantocos.com.

Security

Access to client material is scoped to the people working on the engagement, and the data processing agreement records the security expectations on both sides.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim certifications we do not hold. If a breach affecting your data occurs, you will be told without undue delay and the notification duties in the agreement will be followed.

Children

This site is written for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 through it.

Accessibility

The site is built to the WCAG 2.2 AA level as a target: semantic headings, keyboard-operable components, visible focus, reduced-motion support and text contrast checked at 4.5:1 for body copy.

A formal third-party accessibility audit has not been carried out, so this is a statement of intent and construction, not a certification. If a page or component blocks you, write to hello@quantocos.com with the page and the assistive technology you use.

Modern slavery statement

Quantocos Global Systems LLP does not tolerate forced labour, debt bondage, child labour or human trafficking in its operations or in the services it buys.

The group is small and does not publish a supplier audit it has not performed. Where an engagement uses an independent delivery partner, the engagement letter records the partner and the standards it is expected to meet, and a concern can be raised at any time at hello@quantocos.com.

This statement is reviewed when the operating structure or the partner list changes.

Changes and contact

This policy is updated when the site, the hosting or the engagement process changes. The date at the top of the page records the current version.

Questions, requests and complaints go to hello@quantocos.com, or by post to the registered address supplied with the engagement letter on request.
