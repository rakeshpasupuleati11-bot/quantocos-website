The model

One team, assembled around the brief.

A pod is a named manager, specialists from the tracks you bought and AI agents that carry repeatable work. The same pod holds the context from one deliverable to the next.

Why it holds together

The brief is written once and reused.

Scope is agreed at Gate 1 and reported against each month, so the work does not restart with every request.

  • A named manager owns the account, not a queue
  • Every deliverable passes internal QA before you see it
  • IP assigns to you under the engagement letter

Named humans, not a ticket desk

How a month runs

Five gates, in the same order every time.

Stages run the month; gates approve each deliverable. The sequence does not change when the work is urgent.

Brief

You describe the outcome; we return a written scope with a credit estimate.

Direction

The pod presents a route at Gate 2, then produces against it.

Internal QA

Work is checked at Gate 3 before you are asked to look.

Your review

Gate 4 is your review window; comments are logged and resolved.

Go-live

Gate 5 releases the files, with source assets handed over.

Can I buy one track without the others?

Yes. Each track has its own catalog and can run as a single-track pod. The credit rate stays the same.

How are the scoped tracks priced?

Interactive, Automation and REMAU are scoped per client. You get a written scope and a monthly figure before anything starts.

What happens when scope changes mid-month?

Changes are priced in credits at the published rate and approved before work begins. Nothing is added silently to an invoice.
