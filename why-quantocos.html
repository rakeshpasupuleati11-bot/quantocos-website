Why Quantocos

One accountable team beats four suppliers.

The argument for GC³ is not that agencies or freelancers are bad. It is that the handoffs between them cost more than most teams measure, and a single pod removes some of those handoffs.

The economics

You can compare the arithmetic before you talk to anyone.

A published credit rate makes the cost of a pool legible, and a written brief makes the amount of work legible. Together they remove the two numbers that usually stay hidden until the invoice.

  • $3,000 for 120 credits, $6,000 for 240, $9,000 for 360
  • One rate of $25 per credit on every pod
  • No setup fee and no exit fee
  • Scoped tracks quoted in writing before work starts

Is a pod cheaper than an agency?

Not necessarily. The claim is transparency and fewer handoffs, not a lower price. A small one-off project is usually cheaper elsewhere.

Can a pod replace an in-house team?

No. It adds capacity and a system; the brand owner and the strategic owner stay inside your company.

What if we already have an agency?

A pod can run alongside an agency, usually on the tracks the agency does not cover. The brief records the boundary so work is not duplicated.
